Dalian Kewei FC is currently competing in the China League Two, and all its players are either former Dalian Renmin FC players or products of Dalian's youth training system, like former Dalian Renmin FC goalkeeper Chong Zhang. Head coach Ting Zhu is also a local legend. The club's colors are the traditional blue that represents Dalian. The deer on the logo comes from the spotted deer often seen along Dalian's Binhai Road. The team currently plays at Field No.1 of the Dalian Football Youth Training Base, which has a capacity of 5,000. When the author wrote this, they were pushing hard for promotion to League One, and in the next four games, they just need to get 5 points to enter the promotion playoffs.
