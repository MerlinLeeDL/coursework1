In 2006, due to certain reasons, the team ran into financial difficulties. At the same time, because of stadium issues, the team had to move to Jinzhou Stadium, 40km away, which greatly reduced ticket revenue. This season, they only finished sixth in the league, and were knocked out early in both the cup and the AFC Champions League.

In 2007, the team finished fifth in the league and got knocked out of the cup early. To ease financial pressure, they had to sell a lot of their key players.

In 2008, due to the loss of key players, the team only ranked tenth in the league, and also got knocked out in the round of 16 in the cup. The team was renamed Dalian Haichang Shide FC.

From 2009 to 2012, the club never ranked higher than eighth in the league, and in cup competitions, they only made it as far as the quarterfinals. At the same time, the city's other team, Dalian Aerbin FC, got promoted from the China League One to the Super League.

In 2012, Shide Group was involved in corruption, and the team owed a huge debt of 12 billion RMB. Even though Arbin FC tried to save Shide FC, they still didn’t pass the Chinese Football Association’s 2013 season entry requirements, and the club announced its dissolution in November 2012. Most of the players joined Arbin FC through free transfers. The first dynasty in Chinese football completely collapsed.

Even though they brought in a lot of veteran players, Dalian's performance didn’t improve. In the 2013 season, Arbin FC only finished 7th in the league and made it to the quarterfinals of the cup.

In 2014, Arbin FC ranked second to last in the Chinese Super League and was disastrously relegated. For the first time, the football city in China lost its top-tier league team.

In 2015, Arbin FC ranked second to last in the Chinese Super League and was disastrously relegated. For the first time, the football city in China lost its top-tier league team.

In 2018, Chinese football, fueled by big money, had already swept through the Super League. Seeing world-class stars in the CSL wasn't surprising anymore. Dalian Yifang Group couldn't support it alone, so Dalian Wanda Group stepped in, buying 20% of Atletico Madrid, 10% of Xie Zhousan, and 50% of Dalian Yifang FC, bringing Carrasco, Gaitan, and Mushekwi from La Liga and the English Championship to the CSL. But because domestic players were relatively weak, the team even fell to the relegation zone at one point. In the penultimate round, thanks to Carrasco’s hat-trick, and in the final round with his assist hat-trick, Dalian managed to stay up, just one point above the drop zone.

In 2019, Dalian Wanda Group bought another 20% of the team's shares, making Wanda Group a real shareholder of the team. The team was renamed Dalian Renmin FC, and the new logo was designed exactly like the first-generation Wanda FC.

In 2020, due to the impact of COVID, the league used a centralized tournament format. In the final round, the team managed a thrilling escape from relegation by coming from behind to beat Shijiazhuang FC, thanks to Liangming Lin scoring twice.

In 2021, due to the loss of key players and a broken cash flow, the team ended up last in the league and got relegated to China League One.

In 2022, the team hired Hui Xie as head coach. Hui Xie's highly aggressive high-press style made the team unstoppable in China League One, earning them promotion to the Chinese Super League as the top team.

In 2023, Hui Xie, making his debut in the Chinese Super League, continued to play aggressively and got some good results at the start of the season. But due to injuries, the team lost the last 8 of their 30 league matches. They finished 11th in the season, successfully avoiding relegation.

In 2024, Hui Xie's tactics had been thoroughly studied by every team. Coupled with an aging lineup and injuries, the team went winless the whole season and ended up second to last, dropping to China League One. By the end of 2024, the team announced it was dissolving due to historical debts, while the newly established Dalian Yingbo FC successfully got promoted.
